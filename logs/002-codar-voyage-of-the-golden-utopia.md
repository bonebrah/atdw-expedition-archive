# Expedition Record

Codar departed Karum Station alone aboard the KRM-01 Golden Utopia, searching the dead worlds for an unknown destination.

His first voyage ended without discovery. After two weeks in the Black, the Golden Utopia emerged at coordinates containing only debris and dust. With nothing to explore, Codar turned back and returned safely to Karum Station.

Before leaving again, Codar underwent additional training and prepared the Golden Utopia for another journey into the unknown.

The second voyage lasted three weeks. During transit, the ship abruptly dropped from warpspace near what appeared to be Karum Station, except Àrsaidh vessels moved around the station as if humanity had never claimed it. The vision vanished almost as quickly as it appeared, and the Golden Utopia returned to warpspace.

Codar eventually arrived at Vli-gat 774198, a large derelict Àrsaidh vessel that had once served as a tomb.

The interior was unstable, tight, cramped, and overrun.

In the first explored area, Codar recovered three small Àrsaidh artifacts: an engraved gold ring, a strange light-emitting sphere, and a stone cube that resisted KSA scanning. He continued deeper into the derelict.

Soon afterward, loud noises echoed through the tomb.

In the third explored area, Codar encountered an Elite Aberration later designated Ar. The creature was cancrine, covered in brightly colored feathers, and moved on six clawed limbs. A beak sat beneath two front-facing eyes.

Codar initially attempted to keep his distance and escape, but Ar rapidly closed the gap. Codar opened fire with his AW&S rifle, repeatedly knocking the creature down and wounding it.

Ar continued to recover.

Its Drain ability proved decisive. Each successful attack inflicted a wound directly while allowing the creature to recover from its own injuries. Codar suffered a severe gash and damage to his ear as the fight continued.

With two wounds already sustained, Codar abandoned the fight and attempted to flee.

Ar caught him before he could escape.

A final Drain attack inflicted Codar's third and fatal wound aboard Vli-gat 774198.

The expedition was never completed.
