# Expedition Record

Ocyd departed Karum Station alone aboard the KRM-01, following the path of the Black Peacock toward an unknown destination.

The voyage proved dangerous before he ever reached the surface. A malfunction depressurized the ship's cabin, forcing Ocyd into his vacc suit while he repaired the damaged systems. He survived, but the long journey and isolation took their toll. By the time he arrived at RT Dictys 3950, he carried new anxiety, trauma, and an increasingly powerful compulsion to protect others.

The world below was scorched, hostile, and active.

Not long after planetfall, a violent tremor sent boulders crashing through the surrounding terrain. Ocyd was badly injured, suffering crushed fingers and brain damage. He managed to treat his hand, but the damage to his brain remained.

Continuing onward, he encountered an extremely active volcano that forced him to spend additional time navigating the region. The surrounding landscape became increasingly convoluted and difficult to cross.

It was there that the expedition ended.

A Taker ambushed Ocyd in the broken terrain. The creature's sticky mucus left him unable to escape. Its first attack missed, but Ocyd was already carrying two serious wounds from the earlier hazard. A later slash knocked him to the ground.

The Taker struck again.

Ocyd suffered his third and final wound on the surface of RT Dictys 3950.

The expedition was never completed.
